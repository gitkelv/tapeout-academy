How this maps to TapeOut

Everything here is simulated in the browser. Each piece lines up with how TapeOut is publicly described, so the team could wire it to real contracts.

01

Transistor tokens

Your TRN balance stands in for TapeOut's transistor tokens (ERC-1155). Every NAND gate costs 4.

02

Tape-out burns, then mints

Taping out uses up the transistors and mints the circuit as an NFT (ERC-721) that records how its gates connect.

03

Composable chips

Taped-out circuits become building blocks. NOT → AND → XOR → adders, the same path that leads to a CPU.

04

Block-clocked compute

The final lesson ticks with each block, echoing TapeOut's 4004-style processor clocked by block production.

What the TapeOut team could build next

  • Swap the simulated wallet for a real X Layer / BNB Chain wallet and call the actual tape-out contract.
  • Turn finished lessons into onchain credentials: proof that someone designed a working adder.
  • Pay royalties to designers when their circuits get reused in someone else's tape-out.
  • Add sequential lessons (latches, registers) and climb all the way to the CPU.
TapeOut Academy is a community concept prototype. Not affiliated with TapeOut. No real tokens or chains are used.
